BidReady Intelligence is an Atlassian Forge demonstration app for reviewing tender requirements, documenting fictional evidence, comparing addenda, and explaining reviewed assessment status. It is intended for hackathon judges, authorized testers and internal evaluation.

By installing or using BidReady, you agree to use it responsibly and only on Atlassian sites where you have authorization.

BidReady is a **decision-support demonstration**, not a procurement authority, legal adviser, insurer, compliance certification service, or bid-submission agent. HOLD, REVIEW and NO-BID results depend on the evidence and source information reviewed in the app; they may be incomplete or wrong. **You are responsible for verifying requirements with the official buyer and making every actual bidding decision yourself.**
